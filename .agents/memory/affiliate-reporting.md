---
name: Affiliate reporting scope
description: Distinguishes SabiSwap affiliate history from the campaign and SideShift-wide activity.
---

The user wants recent completed transactions and all-time volume generated by their affiliate ID below Market Pulse. This is separate from campaign progress and manually approved reward entries.

**Why:** SideShift's documented public recent-shifts feed is platform-wide, and its REST account endpoint reports balances rather than affiliate volume. Neither is a substitute for account-scoped transaction history.

**How to apply:** Keep public activity tied to the configured affiliate account. Use provider-reported historical USD values, not today's crypto prices or commission-derived guesses. If reporting fails, show unavailable or explicitly dated cached data rather than unrelated transactions or a fabricated zero.

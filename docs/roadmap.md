# SabiSwap roadmap

[Documentation home](README.md)

This roadmap outlines SabiSwap's short- and long-term priorities. It describes
product direction, not a fixed delivery schedule. Planned features are not
necessarily available today, and priorities may change.

## Short term

### App development

Continue developing the SabiSwap application, with a focus on reliable crypto
swaps, a clear interface, useful order tracking and a smooth experience across
devices.

### Social media growth

Grow SabiSwap's social presence and community through product updates,
educational content and engagement across its official channels.

### SEO optimization

Improve search visibility through useful documentation, crawlable pages,
accurate metadata and ongoing technical SEO improvements.

### $100k swap campaign

Run the $100,000 swap-volume campaign to encourage product use and community
participation. The $100k figure is a swap-volume goal, not a funded prize pool.
Campaign rules and reward allocations are outside the scope of this documentation.

### Ambassadorship program

Develop an ambassadorship program to help community members introduce SabiSwap
to new users, create educational content and support community growth.
Program details will be announced separately.

## Long term

### NFT minting

Explore an NFT minting experience as part of the SabiSwap ecosystem. Collection
details, minting mechanics and any associated utility remain to be defined.

### Utility/reward token

Explore a utility/reward token for the SabiSwap ecosystem. Token design,
distribution, supported networks and intended uses remain to be defined.
This roadmap is not a token-sale announcement or a promise of an airdrop.

### Staking

Explore staking functionality in connection with future ecosystem development.
Supported assets, technical design, eligibility and any reward mechanics will
be defined before launch. No staking yield or return is promised.

## Follow progress

Future announcements and feature availability will be shared through official
SabiSwap channels:

- [X](https://x.com/sabiswap)
- [Telegram](https://t.me/sabiswap)
- [CoinMarketCap Community](https://coinmarketcap.com/community/profile/SabiSwap)
- [GitHub](https://github.com/digdeepdev/Sabiswap)

NFT minting, a utility/reward token and staking are roadmap items, not currently
enabled app features.
